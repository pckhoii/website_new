# Personal Universe — Design Direction

## Core idea
A personal archive of work, ideas, reading, places, experiments and everyday curiosity.

Professional core:
Data / Analytics / Data Engineering

The site should represent the whole person, not only the profession.

## Visual thesis
70% editorial personal archive
20% data/system aesthetics
10% astronomical metaphor

## Feel
- intelligent
- personal
- atmospheric
- modern
- refined
- visually rich
- calm

## Avoid
- SaaS landing page
- generic AI portfolio
- giant planets
- giant serif text without purpose
- empty hero sections
- excessive cards
- excessive glow
- cyberpunk
- purple AI gradients
- decorative sci-fi labels everywhere

## Image strategy
Every major viewport should contain at least one meaningful visual.

Use:
- data/project screenshots
- dashboard previews
- editorial graphics
- personal/travel photography
- note covers
- reading visuals
- abstract data/system graphics

Temporary demo visuals are allowed until final assets are available.

## Layout
Prefer:
- asymmetric editorial layouts
- mixed content density
- image/text compositions
- hover previews
- strong whitespace
- varied section rhythm

Avoid repetitive 3-column card grids.

## Motion
Use:
- masked image reveal
- subtle parallax
- hover preview
- text reveal
- page transitions
- atmospheric background motion

Motion should support content, not compete with it.
