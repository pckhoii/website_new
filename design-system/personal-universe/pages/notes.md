# Notes — Personal Universe

> Extends `../MASTER.md` for the Notes archive.

## Direction

An editorial archive of essays, field notes, observations, and unfinished thoughts. The page uses a restrained orbit only as atmosphere; entries are structured by date, type, image, title, and excerpt.

## Rules

- Keep Notes personal and exploratory, not a publication dashboard.
- Allow entries to have different visual weights and optional imagery.
- Preserve clear chronological reading order in the DOM.
- Demo cover paths come from `src/lib/content.ts`.
- EN / VI switching and reduced motion behave exactly as on the homepage.
