# Home — Quiet Personal Archive

> Overrides `../MASTER.md` for the homepage.

## Dominant visual

**Orbital Field Notes** — a quiet identity statement paired with an interactive archive field. Work, Notes, Reading, and Places appear as selectable signals on asymmetric orbital paths, while a circular data lens anchors the professional identity without becoming a literal planet. The rest of the page translates that field into a compact editorial index.

## Structure

1. Identity hero — name, role, one personal statement
2. Archive prologue — Notice → Shape → Keep
3. Recent Signals — mixed archive index
4. Selected Work — project index with one contextual preview
5. Notes — visual essays and unfinished thinking
6. Reading — concise shelf index
7. Places — image-led place index
8. About — one short personal introduction

## Narrative spine

**Observe → Build → Think → Keep → Wander → Return**

The homepage is not a sequence of portfolio categories. It is a story about how Khôi's curiosity moves: noticing a signal, turning questions into systems, recording what remains, keeping useful references, letting places change the scale of a question, and returning to the person behind the archive.

- The hero archive field makes the site taxonomy visible and interactive without inventing unfamiliar navigation labels.
- The circular center is always a data lens or archive instrument, never a planet.
- The generated archive-field artwork visualizes raw signals becoming systems and memory. It appears inside the hero lens and expands into the archive prologue.
- The prologue states the personal logic in three movements: notice patterns, shape some into systems, and keep both systems and unanswered questions.
- A restrained desktop chapter rail makes the longer Observe → Return path visible while scrolling; it is removed on compact screens.
- The chapter rail rests as a narrow instrument in the left gutter and expands only on hover or keyboard focus; it must never cover section content.
- Vietnamese travel language uses “Chuyến đi” and “Những nơi mình đã đi qua”, avoiding abstract labels such as “Nơi chốn”.
- Route transitions use one observatory-mount movement: a telescope repoints from coordinate A to destination B, then exits. Avoid atom-like orbit animation.
- The narrative is carried by content order instead of a persistent chapter rail or a separate full-screen thesis section.
- Mobile treats the orbital field as a cropped atmospheric layer in the first viewport, preserving readable copy and a visible circular signature.
- Chapter-aware atmosphere uses only subtle radial light shifts. It must never become a literal space-game environment.

## Rules

- Hero occupies roughly one viewport on desktop and recomposes into a layered first viewport on mobile.
- The primary Explore CTA and accessibility skip link both lead to Recent Signals.
- Navigation uses clear labels: Work, Notes, Reading, Places, About.
- Hero statement uses one controlled serif thought: “Curious by default.” Professional identity remains in the sans-serif role line.
- Hero display type stays within roughly 42–66 px on common desktop viewports and must never dominate the full screen.
- Section headings use restrained serif display type; index and item titles use sans-serif.
- Body copy stays near 16 px with readable line-height and short measures.
- The hero field occupies the right half on desktop and appears as a partially cropped orbital layer on mobile.
- EN / VI text is rendered from one content dictionary and the selected language persists locally.
- Work uses a keyboard, hover, tap-accessible editorial index with one active preview.
- Demo asset paths live in content objects and must remain replaceable without component edits.
- Work rows stay concise; project context appears in the active preview instead of repeating inside every row.
- Recent Signals mixes content types without turning each item into a rounded card.
- Notes, Reading, and Places prioritize visual evidence and titles over repeated explanatory copy.
- Reading may use a warm paper section to create editorial contrast.
- Places introduces restrained amber warmth without changing the core system.
- About contains one concise paragraph; decorative diagrams are removed when they compete with the person behind the archive.
- Motion is limited to reveal, hover preview, pointer response, and slow orbital drift, with a readable reduced-motion state.
- All placeholder personal content lives in `src/lib/content.ts` or obvious page copy.
