# Work Detail — Demo Case Studies

> Extends `../MASTER.md` for routes under `/work/[slug]`.

## Transition concept

**Celestial Aperture** treats navigation as a short opening between chapters of the archive. Three offset orbital planes gather around a small signal core while one outer arc resolves, then the composition clears in roughly one second.

This is intentionally distinct from a clock loader: no time readout, tick marks, four-number arrangement, or neutral gray stage. Its uneven orbital geometry, ink-blue atmosphere, and amber relay point come from Personal Universe's own archive-map language rather than a literal instrument face.

## Case-study structure

1. Project identity, discipline, contribution, and period
2. Existing project visual as a wide editorial plate
3. One working question that frames the case
4. Context in concise narrative form
5. A three-move system/process index
6. Intended qualitative outcomes
7. Explicit demo-content disclaimer
8. Link to the next project

## Interaction rules

- Every row in Selected Work is a real link and retains its hover/focus preview.
- The loader never blocks pointer input and is removed after the transition.
- Reduced-motion users bypass the loader entirely.
- Project visuals may scale very slightly on hover; text and layout stay stable.
- Back and next-project links remain conventional and keyboard accessible.

## Content rules

- The three current pages are illustrative demos, not claims about verified client work.
- Do not invent performance numbers, client names, testimonials, or confidential details.
- Replace demo copy and visuals with verified material before presenting a case as factual.
- Keep data work central while explaining the question, decision layer, and human use—not only tools.
